FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Quick answers for visitors who want to understand the site and where it is headed.

What is this website for?

This is Cian's personal website: a place for a short introduction, future projects, links, and updates.

Why does it look automotive-inspired?

The visual direction uses cues from premium automotive websites: confident photography, direct navigation, strong contrast, and careful spacing.

Is this connected to Porsche?

No. The site is only stylistically inspired by luxury automotive design and does not use Porsche branding, logos, or product imagery.

Can more pages be added?

Yes. The site is plain static HTML and CSS, so more pages like Projects, Contact, Writing, or Resume can be added cleanly.

Where should visitors go next?

The About Me page is the best next stop. It introduces the site and gives room for more personal details later.
